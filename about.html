About us

We build the rate limiter we kept rewriting.

Our mission is simple: no engineering team should have to build, own and debug traffic protection that has nothing to do with their actual product.

How it started

In 2023 two of us were on call for a payments API when a partner's retry loop went wrong and sent 40 million requests in nine hours. Nothing was malicious. Nothing was even unusual - just a backoff bug on their side. Our bill was not amused.

We spent the following quarter building the thing that would have caught it: per key limits, burst allowances, and a view that shows which caller is responsible while it is still happening. Every other team we described it to asked for a copy.

So we turned it into a product. ThrottleBox has been used in production since 2024, and it is still shaped by the same rule - if a policy cannot be read and understood by the engineer on call at 3am, it is the wrong policy.

Values
